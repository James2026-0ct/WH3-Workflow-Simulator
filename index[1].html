Simulation assumptions and limitations

The 2026 scenario releases batches of eight into update, overlaps operator enrollment with machine updating, and permits QC handoffs. A maximum of 32 units may be active in the operator's working window. The 2025 scenario waits for 40 units to be prepared before releasing the batch. Processing durations are editable assumptions, not verified floor measurements. Enrollment is operator-constrained; updates are bench-constrained; QC is independently constrained. No forced outcome is built in.

This is a simplified discrete-event model, not a validated forecast. A defensible Green Belt appendix would require actual preparation, update, enrollment, QC, and transfer timings for both workflows, plus resource availability and observed downtime.
